Out of office

The following receipe shows how to send an automatic answer to incoming messages. Fro this we use the utility program "formmail". We also take some precaution to avoid an infinite loop when the sender also have an automatic reply to avoid mails just bouncing back and forth.

#--------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Out of office
#--------------------------------------------------------------------------
:0 h c
* !^FROM_DAEMON
* !^FROM_MAILER
* !^X-Loop: out_of_office
| (/usr/bin/formail -r -A"Precedence: junk (autoreply)" -A"X-Loop: out_of_office" \
-I"Subject: Out of office reply"; cat $HOME/out_of_office.txt) | $SENDMAIL -t
